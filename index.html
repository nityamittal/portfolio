About Me

Building Intelligent Systems

Hi, I'm Nitya, a machine learning engineer finishing my MS in Computer Science at the University of Michigan. I like taking research-grade ideas and turning them into systems that actually run: low-latency, well-tested, and honest about what they do.

I started in data engineering at American Express, building Python and SQL pipelines for anti-money-laundering systems and saving ~$70K through data rationalization. That taught me to care about reliability, observability, and data you can trust, habits I now bring to everything I build in ML.

Since then I've gone deep on AI: real-time recommenders on Kafka and Redis, retrieval-augmented assistants grounded in verified corpora, and a GPT-style transformer written from scratch in PyTorch. Away from the keyboard I sketch and paint, and I've mentored 300+ students along the way.
